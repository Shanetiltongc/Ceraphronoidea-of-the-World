# {{frontmatter.title}}

These pages present taxa in the superfamily Ceraphronoidea (Hymenoptera), curated in [TaxonWorks](https://taxonworks.org). Source nomenclature includes Johnson & Musetti (2004), *Catalog of Systematic Literature of the Superfamily Ceraphronoidea (Hymenoptera)*.

The catalog covers:

- **Ceraphronidae**
- **Megaspilidae** (Lagynodinae and Megaspilinae)
- **Stigmaphronidae** (extinct)
